# Code of conduct

Be kind, be patient, assume good intent. This is a hobby-sized project with people from many countries and languages.

- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- No sharing of someone's voice, personal data or recordings without their consent.
- Don't ask for help using Grey to impersonate, deceive or defraud anyone: those issues are closed.

Problems? Email support@thisvoiceisai.com. Reports are handled privately.
